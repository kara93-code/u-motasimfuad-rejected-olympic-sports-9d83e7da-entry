# Rejected Olympic Sports — three versions

Contest: u-motasimfuad-rejected-olympic-sports-9d83e7da ("Rejected Olympic Sports").
Judge: comedy writer, pairwise. Criteria in order: funny rejected Olympic
sport / lands in one read with the twist before the line ends / sounds like
a real broadcast event (absurdity from the sport, not random words) /
no wasted words / quotable. Limit: 1–200 characters, one line.

## Version A — Competitive Parallel Parking

> Competitive Parallel Parking: drivers must park in a space exactly 10 cm longer than the car, judged on kerb distance, mirror use, and not crying.

Characters: 146. Daily activity (parking) wrapped as a judged sport with
specific rules (10 cm longer, kerb distance, mirror use). Straight-faced
commentary voice; the twist ("and not crying") lands on the last two words.
Relatable to every driver; quotable.

## Version B — Freestyle Password Creation

> Freestyle Password Creation: athletes invent a password with one capital, one number, one symbol, and no memory of it thirty seconds later.

Characters: 139. Daily activity (making a password) with the exact rules
everyone knows (capital, number, symbol). The twist is the punchline of the
whole ritual: you forget it immediately. Shortest of the three, one read,
no wasted word.

## Version C — Speed Fitted-Sheet Folding

> Speed Fitted-Sheet Folding: two athletes, one fitted sheet, ninety seconds — and the elastic corners are allowed to fight back.

Characters: 127. Daily activity (folding a fitted sheet) as a timed duel
with a named opponent: the elastic corners. The sheet itself fights back,
which is why the Olympics would turn it down. Shortest, most visual,
twist in the last three words.
